Experience

A Balkan Steakhouse After Dark

KAJMAK begins as a steakhouse and transforms as the night deepens. The sound rises, the lights dim, rakia reaches the table, shisha service joins the room, and the evening becomes something between dinner, celebration, and Balkan ritual.

The Dining Room

Prime steakhouse service with Balkan soul.

Dinner at KAJMAK starts with the discipline of a luxury steakhouse: polished service, prime cuts, fire-grilled technique, sharp cocktails, and a room designed for lingering.

The Balkan character is felt in the details: kajmak melting into warm bread, ajvar on the table, rakia poured with intention, and generous plates that invite the whole table in.

The Kafana Energy

A room that knows how to celebrate.

The spirit of a Balkan kafana is warm, musical, generous, and alive. At KAJMAK, that energy is refined through a New York lens: elegant tables, dark lighting, premium bottles, and a soundtrack that rises with the room.

Birthdays, reunions, late dinners, and spontaneous toasts all belong here.

The Supper Club

Dinner becomes the night.

As service moves later, the atmosphere turns cinematic: deeper lights, louder music, more champagne, more rakia, and the unmistakable charge of a Balkan room in celebration.

Rakia, Shisha & Ritual

The pour and the smoke are part of the evening.

Rakia is not treated as a novelty. It is part of the KAJMAK rhythm: offered in flights, poured tableside, paired with food, and served as a ritual for guests who want the full Balkan night. Premium shisha is available for late-night and VIP tables.

Plum, quince, apricot, pear, grape, honey, and reserve selections bring the region to the table.
